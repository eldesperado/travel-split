Travel Split · UX Design · v1.0

User Journeys &
Use Case Priorities

Two actor journeys, three main flows, four sub-flows. Designed so the organizer can record an expense in 4 taps and the member can read their settlement in 2 seconds.

Section 1

Use Case Priorities

● Main flows — critical path

Always visible. No extra steps. User finishes the job in the fewest possible taps.

UC-1
Add trip members
Type a name, tap Add. Prerequisite for everything else. Auto-focused on first launch.
First action
UC-2
Record an equal-split expense
Title → Amount → Paid by → Save. 4 fields. Default split is always equal across all members. No extra decision required.
Golden path — 4 taps
UC-4
Review balances and settle up
Net balance per person with ▲/▼ indicator (not color-only). Suggested payments with receipt-style explanation. Updates live as expenses change.
Outcome screen
✦ HIG: Primary actions always in thumb reach. Form fields above the keyboard's safe area. Settlement teaser stays visible at the bottom so the outcome is never hidden during entry.
○ Sub-flows — advanced, optional

Hidden behind disclosure patterns. Power users opt in. Never interrupt the fast path.

UC-3
Weighted custom split
Hidden behind "Customize split" accordion inside the expense form. Lets recorder exclude participants or set weights > 1. Helper text shows current split state at all times.
Disclosure / accordion
UC-5
Edit or delete an expense
Edit/delete icons per row in the expense list. Edit re-opens the form prefilled. Delete requires a second confirm. Settlement updates immediately.
Row actions
UC-6
Remove a person
Remove button blocked (disabled) if person appears in any expense. Tooltip explains why. Unused people can be removed freely.
Guarded action
UC-7
Copy settlement summary
One button copies plain-text settlement to clipboard for sharing via Messages/WhatsApp. No accounts, no server. Local only.
Share affordance
✦ HIG: Destructive actions (delete) are never the default. Blocked states explain themselves inline — never silent failures. Advanced controls live inside disclosure to preserve visual calm.
Section 2

Actor Journeys

🗺️
Actor A
Trip Organizer

The organizer opens the app, builds the group, records expenses during the trip, and shares the final settlement. They interact with the app repeatedly — often at restaurants, museums, or hotels in the moment.

1
Open app → workspace appears
No splash screen, no setup wizard. The People panel is immediately visible with the name input auto-focused.
⚡ 0 taps to reach input
2
Add all travelers by name
Type a name, tap Add, repeat. Order doesn't matter. Names appear as chips in the list. Takes ~5 seconds per person.
⚡ 2 taps per person
3
Record first expense (fast path)
Title → Amount → Paid by → Save. All members included by default with equal weight. Settlement updates immediately after save.
⚡ 4 taps · golden path
4
(Optional) Customize the split
Tap "Customize split" to reveal participant checkboxes and weight inputs. Exclude someone or give Alex weight 2 for eating twice as much. Helper text confirms the split before saving.
○ Sub-flow · opt-in
5
Continue adding expenses throughout trip
Each expense takes 4 taps. The Expenses panel shows a running list. The settlement teaser at the bottom stays visible, previewing the current largest pending payment.
↻ Repeating action
6
(Optional) Correct a mistake
Tap the edit icon on any expense row to open it prefilled. Change fields and save. Or tap delete to remove it. Settlement recalculates instantly.
○ Sub-flow · row action
✓
Review settlement — trip done
Scroll to Settlement (or tap rail). See net balances with ▲/▼. Read suggested payments: "Sam → Mina $163.00". Tap a payment to see the receipt explanation.
⚡ Instant, no calculation needed
7
(Optional) Share settlement summary
Tap "Copy summary" to copy plain text to clipboard. Paste into group chat. Members see who owes what without needing the app.
○ Sub-flow · clipboard share
👤
Actor B
Trip Member

The member is a participant in the trip. They're named in the app but don't necessarily use it themselves. Their journey is about understanding what they owe and to whom — as simply as possible.

Note: This is a local-first, no-account app. Members don't "log in." They receive their settlement info via a shared summary text, or by looking at the organizer's device.
Path A — views the app
1
Organizer hands over device or screen-shares
Member opens the app on the organizer's device — or the organizer shares their screen.
2
Navigate to Settlement section
Tap "Settle" in the anchor rail. Immediately sees their name in the net balance list with a clear ▼ indicator and amount.
⚡ 1 tap
✓
Read who to pay and how much
E.g. "Sam → Mina $163.00". Tap the row to see receipt explanation: "This clears your −$163.00 balance." Member knows exactly what to do.
⚡ 2 seconds to understand
Path B — receives copied summary text
1
Organizer copies & pastes settlement to group chat
Plain text: each person's balance and each suggested payment. No app required to read.
✓
Member reads: pay $163 to Mina
Message is self-explanatory. Member pays via their preferred method (Venmo, bank transfer, cash) outside the app.
⚡ No app install needed
✦ Design implication: The settlement view must be readable in one glance. Non-color indicators (▲/▼) and the receipt explanation text mean a member never needs to "figure out" what they owe.
Section 3

Key Design Decisions

Expense entry default
4 fields only — title, amount, paid by, save. Equal split across all members is automatic. No checkbox required. Customization is opt-in via disclosure.
Advanced split access
Disclosure accordion labelled "Customize split". Collapsed by default. A split-hint badge ("Split equally across 4 people") always confirms the current state so the organizer never wonders what will happen.
Mobile navigation
Sticky anchor rail (People | Expenses | Settle) at top, not bottom, to avoid conflict with iOS home indicator. Bottom reserved for the settlement teaser — a persistent reminder of the current suggested payment.
Settlement trust signal
Receipt card on tap: explains each payment in plain language, not just numbers. "This clears Sam's −$163.00 balance." Members never doubt the math.
Person removal guard
Disabled remove button with tooltip when person is in any expense. Prevents orphaned references. The button is visible but grayed — the organizer understands the constraint without a modal.
Color + non-color cues
▲ / ▼ / ◦ indicators alongside green/red amounts. WCAG AA compliant. Users who can't distinguish red from green still understand positive vs. negative balances.
First launch experience
No onboarding modal. App opens directly to workspace. People panel name input is auto-focused. The organizer's first action is already correct — no "skip intro" needed.
Share affordance
Copy to clipboard only — plain text settlement. No backend, no invite links, no real-time sync. Members don't need the app. Sharing is the organizer's choice, not the app's assumption.
