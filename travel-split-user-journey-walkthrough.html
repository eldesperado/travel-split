Travel Split · interactive walkthrough

What users are trying to do, and how the app should guide them.

The app is not a banking system or a collaboration network. It is a local travel notebook: add the group, record shared costs, and leave with a clear answer to “who pays whom?”

One sentence

Use case means the job.

A use case is the situation the app must support: start a trip, add an expense, correct a mistake, settle up, or come back later.

One sentence

User journey means the path.

A journey is the sequence of screens, taps, checks, and outcomes someone goes through to finish that use case.

Phase 1 boundary

Everything happens on one device.

No accounts, backend, live invite links, or payment processing. Sharing is only copying a summary out to another app.

What changes on screen
Organizer journey

Creates the trip shape.

  • Adds the roster quickly as chips.
  • Optionally sets “recording as” for convenience.
  • Records the first expense using equal split defaults.
  • Controls roster cleanup: used people cannot be deleted.
  • Can copy a settlement summary to send elsewhere.
Trip member journey

Records or reads the trip state.

  • Uses the same local workspace when holding the device.
  • Can add expenses by choosing who paid.
  • Reviews activity and settlement suggestions.
  • Receives copied summary outside the app if the organizer shares it.
  • Does not join through an account or live link in Phase 1.
